Privacy policy

PM Claude accesses Google data only in the Google accounts of its developer, and only after that person grants access on Google's consent screen.

The data is read and written from the developer's own computer. Access tokens are stored on that computer. Content the developer chooses to work on may be processed by the AI assistant the developer runs there.

No data is collected from anyone else, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 10 October 2026.
